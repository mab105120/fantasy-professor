How to read the numbers. Every stat comes from the 2026 box scores (two games per team; three for ATL and GB), so treat them as early signals, not settled truth. Key terms: pass rate is the share of plays that are dropbacks (passes plus sacks). Sack rate is sacks per dropback; we use it as a stand-in for pass protection because true pressure rates are paywalled. YPC is yards per carry, our run-blocking stand-in. Net Y/A is passing yards minus sack yards, per dropback. FPA (fantasy points allowed) is what a defense has given up to each position in this league's scoring: 4-pt passing TDs, full PPR, −2 per INT and per fumble lost. Target share is a player's cut of his team's targets, and carry share the same for runs. FP consensus is the FantasyPros expert consensus ranking (the average of 37–46 experts, updated 9/26), with its A–F matchup grade.

Glossary (tap to open)
Target share / carry share
A player's slice of his team's pass targets or rushing attempts. The best single predictor of fantasy volume.
Snap share / route share
Percent of offensive plays a player is on the field for, or runs a pass route on. It shows role before the stats catch up.
Sack rate
Sacks ÷ dropbacks. On offense, lower means better protection; on defense, higher means a better pass rush.
Pressure rate / blitz rate
How often the QB is hurried, or how often the defense sends extra rushers. Quoted from ESPN where used.
YPC
Yards per carry.
Net Y/A
(Pass yards − sack yards) ÷ dropbacks. Captures efficiency and protection in one number.
FPA
Fantasy points allowed per game to a position, computed from box scores in D League scoring.
Rush yards over expected
Yards a runner gains compared with what an average back would get on the same carries.
Cover-1
Man-to-man coverage with one deep safety. It rewards receivers who win one-on-one.
12 / 13 personnel
Formations with one RB and two or three TEs.
Handcuff
The backup who would inherit a starter's role if he got hurt.
Line / total
The betting spread and combined-points estimate. Used here only as a read of expected game script (who leads, how much scoring), not betting advice.
FP consensus (ECR)
FantasyPros' average of dozens of expert weekly rankings, with its A–F matchup grade.
