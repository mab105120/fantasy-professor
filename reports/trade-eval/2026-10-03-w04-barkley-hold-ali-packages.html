Barkley: what the evidence says

What's actually wrong

  • No touchdowns. He has 0 TDs and only 2 carries inside the 5-yard line all season. Jalen Hurts' QB sneaks ("tush push") take the goal-line work. Through Week 3 he's PPR RB33.
  • Almost no receiving role. He has 2 targets a game (6 total, 3 catches for 6 yds). That's down from 3.1/gm in 2025 and 7.6 as a rookie. His route share (how often he runs a pass route when the QB drops back) was 42% in W3, while Will Shipley took passing downs. In PPR that costs him points every week.
  • The offense is worse. New OC Sean Mannion's unit is 24th in yards. DeVonta Smith (hamstring) and Goedert (knee) are OUT for W4. PHI ran only 47 plays in W3.
  • Age 29, and 2025 was already a step down. You remember correctly: he finished RB14 per game in 2025 (1,140 rush yds, 4.1 YPC, 9 TDs), after his 2,005-yard 2024.

What's still fine

  • He still owns the job. W3: 72–74% of snaps and 17 of 20 RB opportunities (85%). Bigsby got 2 snaps, Shipley 11–13.
  • He's running well. 5.1 yards per carry is 7th among RBs with 30+ carries, a full yard better than 2025. In healthy games he's had 15 carries for ~83 yds.
  • Week 2 was the stinger, not decline. He had 4 carries before leaving. The W3 MRI was clean and he's on no injury report for W4 vs LAR.
  • TDs should come back some. He scored 13 and 9 TDs the last two years. A lead back with 0 TDs on 85% of the opportunities is the classic regression spot (FanDuel's buy-low case).

Glossary
ROS
Rest of season: value from now to the end of the fantasy playoffs, not points already scored.
ECR
Expert consensus ranking: FantasyPros' average of many analysts' rankings. A wide best-to-worst range means experts disagree.
Trade-value chart
A number per player so a trade's two sides can be added up. Charts overstate 2-for-1 deals because two good bench players don't equal one starter.
Route share
How often a player runs a pass route when his QB drops back. Low for an RB means he's off the field on passing downs, which costs PPR points.
Opportunities
Carries plus targets. The share of a backfield's opportunities is the cleanest measure of who owns the job.
YPC
Yards per carry. It measures efficiency, but it's noisy over small samples.
TD regression
Touchdowns swing a lot from week to week. A lead back with heavy usage and 0 TDs is more likely to score more going forward than to keep scoring zero.
Tush push
The Eagles' QB-sneak play near the goal line. It sends short TDs to Hurts instead of the RB.
Consolidation
Trading two lesser players for one better one. The side getting the best player usually "pays" a little in total value.
