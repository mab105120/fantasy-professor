Projections are ESPN rest-of-season averages per game. If Collins misses W4, Egbuka (≈11) would outscore Jameson Williams (7.6/gm) this week only. That short-term edge disappears once Collins returns.

Why it helps

  • W8 bye relief: HOU is off in W8 along with Olave, Kamara (NO) and Kittle (SF), in the game against Lamar n Order (3-0). Egbuka's bye is W10.
  • Removes the hamstring risk, which tends to linger or recur.
  • Egbuka is a 2nd-year player, so he adds a small amount of keeper value.

What it costs

  • About 4–5 pts/week in the WR2 slot for the rest of the season, including the playoffs.
  • The W10 bye adds Egbuka to Saquon, Harvey and the Eagles D/ST (PHI/DEN), so it creates a new collision.
  • Strategy says WR starters should be "volume over ceiling." Egbuka's volume (6.7 tgt/gm) is weaker than Collins' (10 in his only full game).

Why ALI would say yes

ALI is 1-2, 6th, the bubble seed. They beat us in W3. Their WR group is Egbuka / D. Smith / Nabers (7.2/gm) / Odunze / Wicks / A. Mitchell (IR). That's plenty of bodies with no WR1. They're also an active trader: they already moved Flowers + Concepcion to Steel Giants for Nabers + Wicks + Mitchell, buying a name WR on a dip.

This deal is a clear win for ALI. They get a WR1 at a discount and an easy yes, and it strengthens a team we could meet in the playoffs. There's also a pending pitch to them (Stafford → Hubbard/Henderson from the 09-30 team review). Don't send both at the same time; decide which one you actually want.

Risks & exposure

  • Collins injury: Q, DNP for two weeks. Wednesday's (09-30) Texans report isn't out yet. A limited session Wed–Fri would support holding. A third DNP week would weaken our position, but a grade-1 strain still means weeks, not months.
  • Egbuka QB: Mayfield out at least 3 weeks (earliest W7, possibly W8). Daniels went 0-for-3 with an INT in relief.
  • Byes: Keeping Collins means a heavy W8 (4 starters out). Plan for it with Stafford/Kittle trades already in motion (London's bye is W11), not by selling Collins cheaply.
  • Keeper value: Egbuka (2nd-year) has some. Collins (27) would be a reasonable keeper too under the flat-cost rule. This is roughly a wash.

Glossary
ROS
Rest of season: value from now through the fantasy playoffs, not points already scored.
Trade value
A chart number (FantasyPros, Roto Street Journal) that puts every player on one scale so a trade's two sides can be added up and compared.
Targets / gm
Passes thrown at a receiver per game. It's the best early predictor of PPR scoring, because catches are worth a point each here.
Grade-1 strain
The mildest muscle strain category, usually 1–3 weeks out. Hamstrings are known to re-aggravate.
Keeper value
Worth to next year's team. Here the cost is a flat round-skip, so a young breakout player is cheap to keep.
