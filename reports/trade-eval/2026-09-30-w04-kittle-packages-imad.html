Why it helps

  • Both deals add a starting WR, fixing the hole the waiver wire couldn't fill.
  • If Collins sits (hamstring, still Q), the new WR starts at WR2. We don't have to fall back on J. Williams.
  • Trading Kittle shrinks the Week 8 bye cluster (Olave, Collins, Kamara, Kittle all off that week).

What it costs

  • No TE backup behind Fannin. A waiver TE would be the emergency fill.
  • Fannin is 22 and his 2-TD week may be a spike. If he fades, TE becomes a weak slot.
  • Kittle's upside in the fantasy playoffs goes to a probable playoff team.

Why Imad might say yes

Imad is 2-1 and 4th, a contender who wants to upgrade now. He's rostering two mid TEs (Johnson FP TE11, Kraft TE8, and he benched Kraft for Johnson). He's deep at WR (six rostered) and RB (five). He already offered Evans + Johnson for Kittle, so he values Kittle at about 1500 on RSJ's chart. Both packages sit near that number.

Pitch angle: "You're deep at WR and RB. Kittle is back to full snaps and fixes the one spot you're not solid at."

Risks & exposure

  • London: ESPN lists him ACTIVE. One search result mentioned a "questionable vs Arizona" designation, but it isn't on a current Falcons injury report (likely a stale item). Re-check Wednesday's ATL report before sending.
  • Rice: no 2026 NFL discipline (league ruling, April). The off-field history is still there if new issues come up.
  • Judkins: Sampson can come back in Week 6 and turn it into a committee.
  • Bye weeks: London and Judkins share Week 11 with Fannin. Rice's bye is Week 5, when Collins might still be limited.
  • Kittle: Achilles recovery. He has held up so far, but that risk goes to Imad either way.

Glossary
ROS
Rest of season: value from now on, not points already scored.
FP ROS rank
FantasyPros' Expert Consensus Ranking: an average of many analysts' rest-of-season rankings.
Trade value (RSJ)
Roto Street Journal's chart puts a number on each player so trade sides can be compared. Here, higher = more valuable.
Targets
Passes thrown to a player. The best single predictor of a receiver's PPR output.
Snap share
Share of the offense's plays a player is on the field for. It shows how big his role is.
FLEX
A lineup slot that can hold an RB, WR or TE. We have two.
Keeper
A player kept for next season. Here it costs a flat round-skip, so cheap young breakouts are nearly free to keep.
