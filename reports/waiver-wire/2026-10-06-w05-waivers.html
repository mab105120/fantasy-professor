Glossary
  • Handcuff: the backup to a starting RB on the same NFL team, held as insurance in case the starter gets hurt.
  • Target share / carry share: a player's percentage of his team's targets or carries.
  • Rolling waivers: claims are awarded in priority order, and a team that wins a claim drops to last.
  • Stream (D/ST): picking up a defense for one good matchup instead of holding one all season.
  • Regression: a stat pulled up by luck (e.g., 3 TDs on 14 carries) tends to fall back toward normal.
