FLEX and WR2: the close calls

Four players sit within about 0.5 projected points of each other: Worthy 11.4 · Tuten 11.0 · J. Williams 10.9, with Kittle at 12.7 a bit clear. When projections are this tight, they don't decide the pick. Role, matchup, and ceiling do.

  • Xavier Worthy (bench). He looks like the "safe" option: KC −11.5 at MIA, and he led KC receivers with 69 snaps and a 19% target share. But in Week 2 he caught 5 passes for just 25 yards, and he's averaging 5.4 yards per catch this season. Tyquan Thornton is taking the deep targets. His Week 2 fantasy line (13.5) was saved by a short TD. Consensus has him WR29, a fringe flex. His role is now floor-only, and as an underdog we want the player who can have a big week.
  • Jameson Williams (start). Two quiet weeks are why experts call him a buy-low. DET vs NYJ is a better scoring spot, and he's the one player in this group who can score 25.
  • Tuten over Worthy/Kamara (start). A lead back's touches beat a receiver with a low catch rate. Kamara (9.6, consensus RB33) is behind Etienne for touches.

Honest disagreement: ESPN's projection slightly favors Worthy. I'm going against it on usage quality, and the gap is 0.5 points either way.
