Glossary

Toss-up band
How far this season's projected matchup margins typically missed the real result (~37 points). A projected edge smaller than that is close to a coin flip.
Tier
A group of players whose projections are close enough that ESPN can't reliably order them. Here, that's within about 3 points.
Usage / volume
Carries and targets (passes thrown to a player). They swing less from week to week than fantasy points do, so they're a steadier tie-breaker.
