Glossary

FLEX
A lineup slot any RB, WR or TE can fill — we have two.
Consensus rank (ECR)
The average of many analysts' weekly rankings; steadier than any single projection.
Target
A pass thrown to a player; in PPR, targets are the best predictor of catches and points.
Pass-funnel defense
A defense that stops the run well, so opponents throw more — good for the QB facing it.
Toss-up band
How far off ESPN's projected matchup margin has been this season; a projected lead smaller than it means nothing.
