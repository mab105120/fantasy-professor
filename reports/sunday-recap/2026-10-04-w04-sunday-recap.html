Nico Collins owns this week. He catches 7 of 8 targets for 118 yards and two touchdowns, scoring 30.8 points on a 15.3 projection, and he does it in a Houston loss to Dallas. On the other side of that game, Dak Prescott throws 45 times and completes 32 for 335 yards and one touchdown, worth 18.1 points (18.1 against a 17.3 projection). Under this league's 4-point passing TD scoring, that's a good-not-great QB day. Chris Olave puts last week's unconfirmed hamstring chatter to rest: 12 targets, 8 catches, 116 yards, 19.6 points. Targets are the passes thrown a receiver's way. Twelve means the Saints are clearly still treating him as their top option.

The supporting cast does its job. George Kittle scores again from the FLEX spot (4 catches, 70 yards, TD, 17.0). Harold Fannin Jr. turns only 3 catches into 11.7 thanks to a touchdown. The Browns defense sacks Pittsburgh 5 times and picks off 2 passes, beating its projection at 8.0 even after allowing 24 points. Derrick Henry gets plenty of work (23 carries) but only 73 yards (3.2 per carry), and his touchdown is what lifts him to 15.9.

The one gut-punch came on the Eagles' first drive. Saquon Barkley pulls up on a first-quarter run against the Rams with a hamstring injury and doesn't return. He finishes with 2 carries for 8 yards and a 2-yard catch, scoring 2.0 against a 14.3 projection, and Philadelphia loses 24-20. In hindsight, Alvin Kamara (22.8) and RJ Harvey (19.3) both put up big games on our bench. That's color, not a second-guess: the lineup was set on the information available before kickoff, and the margin held anyway.

Injury flag: Saquon Barkley (RB, PHI). Hamstring injury on a first-quarter run against the Rams; he didn't return. NFL.com reports he's considered week to week and avoided a long-term injury. Lamar n Order came in with the highest projection in the league (137.3) and posted the week's lowest score (80.78). Nearly every starter came in under projection. Jaxon Smith-Njigba gets only 6 targets (5 catches, 76 yards, 12.6 against 21.9). Trey McBride catches 7 of 12 targets for just 31 yards (10.1 against 17.4). Davante Adams turns 9 targets into 4 catches for 32 yards (7.2), and David Montgomery runs 10 times for 24 yards (4.3). The Chiefs D/ST adds 1.0 after allowing 27 points to Las Vegas. Their best performance of the day, Rhamondre Stevenson's 18.4, sat on the bench.

On ALI's side, Chuba Hubbard runs wild: 20 carries, 122 yards, 2 TDs, 25.9 against 16.6. Malik Nabers adds 6 catches, 112 yards, and a TD (23.2). That carries ALI past Jacoby Brissett's three interceptions (10.44, at −2 per INT in this league) and Emeka Egbuka's 3.3 (2 catches, 13 yards).

Fantasy impact: Lamar n Order's perfect start is over, and they drop out of a share of first. ALI gets back to .500. Hubbard was a centerpiece of our Barkley trade packages before the HOLD decision, and his value just went up in ALI's eyes. Keep in mind his Week 5 bye (per the ledger). Jeremiyah Love (Lamar's RB) ran 14 times for 63 yards and now carries an ESPN QUESTIONABLE tag. Nothing confirms an in-game injury, so treat the tag as unexplained for now.

CeeDee Lamb puts on a show against Houston: 21 targets, 17 catches, 189 yards, and a touchdown, for 41.3 points on a 16.9 projection. Full PPR rewards exactly this kind of volume. Bijan Robinson adds 145 rushing yards and 2 TDs (27.7). Rookie Carnell Tate catches 9 passes for 145 yards (21.5, minus a lost fumble). Jared Goff throws for 412 yards but only one touchdown (20.48), which is how 4-point passing TDs cap even a monster yardage day. Get Hooked's bench was nearly as loud: Kyle Monangai (30 carries, 146 yards, 2 TDs, 28.0), Drake Maye (26.2) and T.J. Hockenson (24.9) all sat.

Bower Outage posted the week's third-highest score and still lost. Kyren Williams catches 10 passes on 13 targets for 67 yards on top of 80 rushing yards and 2 TDs (36.7 against 13.1). Emanuel Wilson handles 21 carries plus 4 targets in Seattle's backfield (27.0 against 11.5), with Zach Charbonnet OUT and Jadarian Price on IR. Brock Bowers adds 20.6. Garrett Wilson's 3 catches for 27 yards (5.7) is the hole that loses it.

Fantasy impact: Get Hooked now leads the league in points for (590.1), 29.5 clear of Lamar n Order and 50.7 clear of us. They're the strongest 3-1 team on paper and the main threat to our top seed. Bower drops to 1-3 despite real production. Emanuel Wilson's volume is worth tracking: if Seattle's injured RBs stay out, he has a workhorse role on a team whose owner has a crowded injury list (Achane, Brown, Price, and Tyson are all unavailable).
