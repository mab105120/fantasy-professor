Get Hooked's two wideouts put up 70.5 points between them and that's the whole story. Amon-Ra St. Brown feasted on Thursday night — 9 catches on 13 targets, 142 yards, two scores (35.2 on 20.4) — and then CeeDee Lamb one-upped him Sunday with 8/153/2 and the record-breaking catch (35.3 on 18.0). The Patriots D/ST chipped in 20 with a defensive touchdown, a pick, a fumble and four sacks while holding Pittsburgh to 3. Drake Maye (8.0) and Bijan Robinson (11.1 — 72 rushing yards, no score, in a 34-3 Falcons humiliation) were the only starters meaningfully short.

ALI got a DeVonta Smith explosion — 10 catches on 13 targets for 117 and a TD (27.7 on 14.0) with Barkley and Goedert both sidelined, exactly the target-funnel you'd expect — and a steady Jahmyr Gibbs (23.3). But Ashton Jeanty ground out 21 carries for 48 yards (10.3 on 18.3), Rome Odunze was buried in the Bears' 3-point offense (7.3), and the Bucs D/ST gave up 23 to Cleveland.

Fantasy impact
ALI trails by 35.6 with Jaxson Dart and Isaiah Likely (combined 29.1 projected) still to go against Tyler Warren (11.4). They need roughly a 36-point net swing from a rookie QB and a TE on Monday night — possible in a Rams-Giants shootout, not likely. Get Hooked is the favorite to reach 2-0 and, at ~144, likely posts the week's high score. Lamb and St. Brown are now the two most expensive WRs in this league's trade market; nobody's prying them loose cheaply.

Josh Allen did Josh Allen things in a 41-31 Thursday-night win over Detroit: 248 passing yards, 3 TD passes, and 14 carries for 69 yards and two rushing scores — 40.8 points, nearly double his 22.7 projection and the top QB score of the week in this league. Christian McCaffrey added 22.6 on two goal-line touchdowns (10 carries for 23 yards, but who's counting). And then the rest of the lineup went dark: DJ Moore finished at −0.1 after leaving Thursday's game with a shoulder/AC-joint sprain, Goedert 1.4 after his knee injury, Javonte Williams 8.0, Etienne 7.1, McLaurin 7.0, Deebo 6.5. Two stars, seven zeros.

Texas Two Step needed a bounce-back and instead ran straight into the Bears-Vikings 9-3 game. Justin Jefferson caught 3 of 6 for 55 (8.5 on 18.4), Aaron Jones 10.5, and Kyle Pitts caught nothing that mattered (2.5) while Atlanta was being run out of its own building. Omarion Hampton (17.5) and Tee Higgins (14.5) beat their numbers, but there just wasn't enough on the board.

Fantasy impact
Texas Two Step trails by 24.3 with only Patrick Mahomes tonight. In 4-point-TD scoring that's a 300-yard, three-touchdown night just to get level — about 6 points above his projection. Redemption is the lean to go 2-0, but they're carrying real damage: DJ Moore is uncertain for Week 3 with the AC-joint sprain (Bills' own report says "not serious"), and Goedert's knee is unresolved. Texas also has Jayden Daniels on the bench now listed DOUBTFUL after his elbow injury in Dallas. If Texas falls to 0-2, that's an owner who drafted for a title and may get antsy — worth a look next week on the trade-leverage board.

This is the game of the week and it's not close to decided. Jaxon Smith-Njigba just authored the league's top line: 9 catches on 11 targets, 155 yards and a career-high three touchdowns — including an 82-yard bomb from Drew Lock (starting for an injured Sam Darnold) on Seattle's second offensive play — good for 42.5 points against an 18.5 projection in a 31-7 blowout of Arizona. The Panthers D/ST, of all things, added 24 with three interceptions, two fumble recoveries, two sacks and a defensive TD while holding Atlanta to 3. Stefon Diggs caught two touchdowns from a Commanders offense that lost Jayden Daniels (21.7), and Trey McBride (18.1) got Arizona's only score. The cost: Lamar Jackson himself (14.8, one TD, one pick in the upset loss to New Orleans) and both running backs — David Montgomery 4.4, Rhamondre Stevenson 3.6 with a lost fumble.

Playoff Chase answered with depth instead of a single haymaker: James Cook 20.9, Ja'Marr Chase 7 catches for 75 and two scores (26.5), Sam LaPorta 17.2 in Thursday's shootout, and Jaylen Waddle — now a Bronco — 8 for 138 (21.8 on 12.2). Jalen Hurts threw the 92-yard pick-six that gifted Tennessee its first points and finished at 16.2, below projection.

Fantasy impact
3.1 points separate them. Playoff Chase has Jonathan Taylor tonight against Kansas City (18.4 proj); Lamar n Order waits until Monday for Davante Adams against the Giants (15.5). The pre-game projections land within 0.2 of each other — this is a genuine coin flip that decides whether Lamar n Order goes 2-0 or Playoff Chase escapes an 0-2 hole. Watch Taylor's workload tonight: if the Colts are chasing points at Arrowhead, his receiving role decides it.
