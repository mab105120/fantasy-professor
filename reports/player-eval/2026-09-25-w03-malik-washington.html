Two regressions that cancel out

His 13.7 yards per catch will fall back toward the 7–9 range of his career. His career is built on jet sweeps, quick screens and gadget looks (CBS/RotoWire), so he doesn't get downfield. His 54% catch rate will climb back toward his 71% 2025 rate. The net: expect roughly 4–5 catches for 35–45 yards on a typical 6-target day, about 8–9 points in our scoring. That matches ESPN's 9.73 projection.
