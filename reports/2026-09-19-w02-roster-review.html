2 · The structural problem: RB-heavy, WR-thin

Seven running backs for two RB slots plus two flexes is more than you can start; four healthy receivers for two WR slots plus those same flexes is fewer than you'd like. The fix is not the wire — the 600-player pool has nothing at WR better than what you hold (best available: Reed 10.2, Shaheed 9.0, Jeudy 8.2). The fix is a trade that converts surplus RB depth and Stafford into a WR2/3.

Why Stafford is a chip, not a drop: Lamar n Order, Redemption team and Bower Outage each roster exactly one QB. To them, Stafford is starting-caliber insurance for Lamar / Josh Allen / Burrow. To you, he's a platoon partner worth ~1 point a week. Same player, very different value — that gap is what a trade captures.
