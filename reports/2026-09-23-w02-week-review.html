The artificial turf in Nashville read 157°F. Philadelphia ran long drives in the first half and led 14–10, but Jalen Hurts' two interceptions turned into 10 Tennessee points and a 20–17 Titans lead with 1:56 left. The injury that matters to us: Saquon Barkley took a stinger on his first carry and missed most of the first half. A stinger is a pinched or stretched nerve in the neck/shoulder that causes arm numbness and is usually short-lived. He returned for the second half but finished with 4 carries for 9 yards. Per Yahoo and ESPN, an MRI was scheduled, and the Eagles don't play until Monday at Chicago, which gives him an extra day. Dallas Goedert hurt his knee in the second quarter.
