Glossary
ROS
Rest of season: value from now until the fantasy playoffs, not points already scored.
Target share
The share of a team's pass attempts thrown to a player. It's the most stable predictor of a receiver's volume.
Route rate
How often a player runs a pass route when his QB drops back. A high rate means he's a real part of the passing game.
